**Pages open the way apps open on a phone.**

**A table opens out of its card.** Tap a table and its card grows into the order page, over a softened floor; go back and the page shrinks into the card again, and the floor comes forward sharp. The Add dishes button, a bill, a row in More: every page opens from the thing you tapped and closes back into it, at one pace, with no bounce.

**Smooth on any phone.** The page beneath is a picture while another moves over it, so nothing is built or measured mid-move; what arrives while a page is moving waits until it has landed. The move starts within a tenth of a second of the tap and runs at the phone's full frame rate, whatever that is.

**One note for the kitchen.** Type a note while adding dishes and it prints under the KOT. The note for the whole order lives in the builder now; the old menu entry is gone.

**Table cards match the counter.** A taken table wears its person's colour down the left edge and as a thin line round the card, as the counter draws it.

**Small things.** A paired phone is never signed out by a stale cloud token; it asks the counter again. Rows from the database no longer fade twice.
