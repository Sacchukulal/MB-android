**Sub-tables, clearer floor cards, and safer order updates.**

**Separate parties at one table.** On a counter that supports sub-tables, add a new party and open each party's order separately. Table details stay in sync with the counter, and occupied cards show the staff member's colour around the border.

**Reduce a kitchen quantity with a reason.** On a supporting counter, permitted staff can reduce a quantity already sent to the kitchen. The request keeps the original line and the reason so retries and changed orders are handled safely.

**Offline orders stay separate.** Queued dishes and kitchen requests keep their link to the order that opened them, including after an app restart or upgrade. The database upgrade preserves existing data.

**More bill history.** The phone reads archived day files, bringing the whole bill history in newest first.
