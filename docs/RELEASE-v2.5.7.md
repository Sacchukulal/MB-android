**The move is smooth now.**

**Pages open and close without a stutter.** The phone no longer copies the screen to a picture every time a finger lands on a card — even to scroll — nor again as every page opens. A page opens out of the card you tapped and closes back into it at the phone's full frame rate; the floor beneath rests while a page sits over it. Coming back, the page you left crossfades into what it is now: a dish you just sent is there as the order page lands, not a moment later.

**Table cards float on a soft shadow.** No border, no stripe; the waiter's colour is on their name.

**One way to show money.** Every rupee figure on the phone — the floor, the order, the menu, Home, Reports, the charts — comes through one formatter: Indian grouping, the same minus sign, whole rupees on the tiles and two decimals everywhere else.

**Small things.** A page cannot be opened twice by a double tap. A page opened from an icon button grows out of that button, never from somewhere you tapped earlier.
