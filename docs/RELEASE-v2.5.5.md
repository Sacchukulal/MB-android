**The floor keeps up, and the screens move as one.**

**Orders reach every phone the moment they happen.** The live line applies what the counter sends in the order it was sent, takes a fresh look at the floor every time it reconnects, and reconnects at once when the phone changes WiFi. A counter that restarts, or a WiFi blip, no longer leaves a floor that needs a swipe to refresh.

**Screens slide, never blend.** Opening a table slides its page in over the floor; going back slides it away. Nothing is seen through anything else, and the floor no longer shifts while the tab bar leaves.

**Settle sends you back to the floor.** Choose Cash, Card or UPI and the phone returns to the tables as the counter is asked.

**The keyboard never hides what you are typing.** Sign-up, the note for the kitchen, the reason for taking a dish off: the field you are in stays above the keyboard.

**Small things.** A table's page opens with its name at once. The Live badge stays on the floor, so "Table 12" is never cut short.
