**Sign up inside the app, and the phone signs itself in.**

**A "Create an account" button on the sign-in screen.** It opens magicbill.in's own sign-up in a window inside Magic Bill — no browser, no address bar, no leaving the app. The account, the plan, the payment and the licence key are all made there, on the real site.

**Straight to the plans.** The window goes to the plan cards as soon as the account is made. Monthly Pro or Yearly Pro, paid through Razorpay — UPI, cards and netbanking, and a UPI app opens and comes back the way it does anywhere else.

**The phone signs itself in.** The moment the shop has its licence, the window closes onto the licence key with a Copy button — type it into Magic Bill on the counter's computer — and this phone is already signed in as the owner behind it. The key stays on Account › Licence.
