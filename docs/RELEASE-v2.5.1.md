**Smaller table cards, and a phone that knows who it is.**

**Three tables to a row.** The cards are smaller — three across on a phone, as many as fit on a tablet — and still say the section, the number, the money, whose it is, the items, the timer and the seats. The one coloured edge stays.

**A role changed at the counter reaches the phone.** The phone asks the counter who it is every time it comes to the front, every time the live line reconnects, and when you pull down on Orders — not only when it was installed. The Account screen shows the role by its name.

**Why the phone has no cloud login, in the counter's own words.** When the counter cannot sign the phone in — no licence key on the counter yet, the cloud out of reach — the Account screen shows the counter's sentence instead of a guess.

**Sign out from any phone.** A phone that is only paired with a counter can sign out too; it no longer needs a cloud login to find the button.
