**The plan is the door.**

**A shop whose plan is not running shows why, not its numbers.** When the plan has ended, run out, or was never paid for, Home and Reports show the reason in one sentence, a button to magicbill.in, and Check again. The moment the plan is paid for, the shop comes back on its own.

**A cancelled plan runs to the day you paid for.** Cancelling switches renewal off at once; the app says "runs until" that day, and stops the day after. A payment that is late gets its grace days, and the app says until when.

**One word for the plan's state, from the cloud.** Active, grace, trial, trial ended, ending, ended, expired: the same word on the phone, on the website and on the counter.
